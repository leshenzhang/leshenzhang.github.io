Privacy Policy

Application: Leshen (Google OAuth desktop client) · Last updated: 4 November 2026

What this application is

Leshen is a personal, single-user desktop application. It is an rclone OAuth client that its author, Leshen Zhang, uses to copy his own research files between his own computers and his own Google Drive account. It is not a product, it is not offered to other people, and it has no users other than the author.

What data it accesses

After the author signs in and grants access, the application reads and writes files in his own Google Drive. It accesses no other Google service and no other person's data.

What data it collects, stores, or shares

None. Specifically:

  • It collects no personal information from anyone.
  • It has no server, no backend, and no analytics. It runs locally on the author's own machines.
  • It transmits file contents only between the author's machines and Google's own API endpoints.
  • It does not sell, share, transfer, or disclose any data to any third party.
  • It does not use any data for advertising, profiling, or training machine-learning models.

The only credential it keeps is the OAuth token Google issues, stored in a local configuration file on the author's own computers and readable only by his own user account.

Retention and deletion

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to retain or delete on our side. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party apps & services, which immediately invalidates the stored token.

Limited Use disclosure

This application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
